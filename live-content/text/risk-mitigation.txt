Risk mitigationMarket pairing reviewPMFI checks that paired markets represent compatible event exposure before execution.Markets may be skipped if event wording, expiry, settlement rules, or resolution logic are not compatible.VWAP-based execution checksPMFI checks executable order book depth instead of relying only on top-of-book prices.This helps reduce the risk of entering trades that look profitable only at a small displayed size.Second-leg recovery logicIf the second leg does not immediately fill, PMFI can retry execution, refresh the order book, and complete the hedge if the trade remains profitable.Unwind is treated as a last-resort option.Liquidity haircutThe strategy does not assume full venue liquidity is safely fillable.By default, it uses only a fraction of the thinner leg’s liquidity:deployable_size = min(bottleneck_liquidity × ARB_FILLABLE_FRACTION, ARB_MAX_PAIR_USDC)Deposit PPS floorDeposits are processed at the higher of current PPS or the initial $1.00 PPS.depositPPS = max(currentPPS, INITIAL_PPS)This prevents below-$1 deposit entry and protects existing holders from dilution.Report postponement policyPMFI may postpone reporting when reported assets are unexpectedly higher or lower than expected and no user deposits or redemptions require immediate processing.Redemption liquidity waterfallPMFI prioritizes available liquidity before touching open positions:Vault idle USDCServicer wallet USDCPolymarket USDC balanceKalshi balanceOpinion balanceSettled venue proceedsNo routine forced sellingPMFI does not normally sell open arbitrage positions before resolution date.Positions are generally expected to resolve through settlement.Stale quote filterThe strategy skips opportunities if the quote is older than the configured stale quote threshold.Capital capsPMFI applies per-pair and total deployed capital caps.High-water-mark feesPerformance fees are charged only on profit above the previous high-water mark.No performance fee is charged during losses or recovery below the high-water mark.Async request cancellationIf a deposit or redeem request remains pending longer than:MAX_REQUEST_AGE = 30 daysthe request owner may cancel the request.
