pArbitrage VaultThe pArbitrage Vault is an automated USDC vault for prediction market arbitrage.Users deposit USDC and receive pARB vault shares after their deposit request is processed by a vault report.The vault strategy searches for paired YES and NO exposure across supported prediction market venues. If pricing conditions are met, the strategy may open a paired position.Core mechanismIn binary prediction markets, YES + NO settle to $1 together.When the combined cost of YES + NO falls below $1 after fees and execution buffers, the vault can capture the spread.What users holdUsers hold pARB shares.Users do not directly hold or manage individual prediction market positions.
